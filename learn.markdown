<p>TVGS is dedicated to helping everybody create games! Our mission is to encourage and enable everyone to make
    games, and we have taught a number of free lessons and skill-building activities over the years to support
    aspiring game makers. This page collect videos, slides, examples, and other resources from previous TVGS
    tutorials. Whether you’re a beginner or a game development expert, we hope you will find some useful info below
    that can help you continue to hone your creative skills.</p>
